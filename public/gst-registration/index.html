Eligibility

Who needs GST registration?

Registration is mandatory in these cases, and voluntary for anyone else who wants to claim input tax credit.

  • Turnover above the threshold₹40 lakh for goods and ₹20 lakh for services in most states (lower in special category states).
  • Inter-state supply of goodsSelling goods to customers in another state requires registration regardless of turnover.
  • E-commerce sellersSuppliers selling through marketplaces such as Amazon or Flipkart, and e-commerce operators.
  • Casual & non-resident taxable personsBusinesses supplying occasionally in a state where they have no fixed place of business.
  • Reverse charge & agentsPersons liable to pay tax under reverse charge, agents of suppliers and input service distributors.
  • Voluntary registrationSmaller businesses can register voluntarily to claim input tax credit and win larger clients.

FAQs

GST Registration — frequently asked questions

How much does GST registration cost?

The government charges no fee for GST registration. Bizpole’s all-inclusive fee is ₹1,500, including GST — there are no other charges.

How long does GST registration take?

You get an ARN as soon as the application is filed. The GSTIN is typically issued in 3–7 working days, longer if the officer raises a query or orders physical verification.

What is the turnover limit for GST registration?

Registration is mandatory once aggregate turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for goods or ₹20 lakh for services in most states. In special category states the limits are lower (₹20 lakh and ₹10 lakh).

Is GST registration mandatory for online sellers?

Yes, in most cases. Suppliers selling goods through e-commerce marketplaces must register irrespective of turnover, with limited exceptions.

Can I register for GST voluntarily?

Yes. Any business can register voluntarily to claim input tax credit and to work with customers who require a GSTIN.

What is the penalty for not registering?

A business that should have registered but did not can be penalised 10% of the tax due or ₹10,000, whichever is higher — and 100% of the tax due for deliberate evasion.

What do I need to do after registration?

Issue GST-compliant invoices and file returns — GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B monthly or quarterly (or CMP-08 under composition) — plus the annual return where applicable.
