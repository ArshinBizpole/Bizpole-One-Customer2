Trademark registration process

How to register a trademark in India — 6 simple steps

We handle every step with the Trade Marks Registry. You get updates on WhatsApp and in your dashboard.

  1. Trademark search

    We check your brand name and logo against existing marks to spot conflicts before you file.

    Same day

  2. Class selection & documents

    Our expert picks the right classes and prepares your Power of Attorney and user affidavit.

    Day 1

  3. Filing (Form TM-A)

    Application is filed online. You receive the application number and can start using ™.

    24–48 hours

  4. Examination

    The examiner reviews your application. If there's an objection, we draft and file your reply within 30 days.

    Typically 1–3 months

  5. Journal publication

    Your mark is published in the Trade Marks Journal and open for opposition for 4 months.

    4-month window

  6. Registration certificate

    If unopposed, the certificate is issued and you can use the ® symbol. Valid for 10 years.

    ® unlocked
Benefits

Why register your trademark?

A registered trademark is a legal asset — it protects your brand, adds value to your business and stops copycats.

Exclusive legal rights

Only you can use the mark for your goods or services across India, and you can sue for infringement.

Stop copycats

Prevent competitors from using a confusingly similar name or logo on marketplaces and social media.

A valuable business asset

Trademarks can be sold, licensed or franchised, and add to your company's valuation.

Marketplace brand registry

A registered or applied mark helps you enrol in Amazon Brand Registry and similar programmes.

Customer trust

The ™ and ® symbols signal an established, legitimate brand to buyers and investors.

Base for global protection

An Indian application can be used as the basis for international filing under the Madrid Protocol.

FAQs

Trademark registration — frequently asked questions

How much does trademark registration cost in India?

The government fee for e-filing Form TM-A is ₹4,500 per class for individuals, startups and small enterprises, and ₹9,000 per class for all other applicants. Bizpole's professional fee is ₹2,999 per class including GST.

How long does trademark registration take?

You get your application number within 24–48 hours of filing and can use the ™ symbol immediately. If there is no objection or opposition, the registration certificate is usually issued in 12 to 24 months.

When can I use the ® symbol?

You can use ™ as soon as your application is filed. The ® symbol can only be used after the Trade Marks Registry issues your registration certificate.

How long is a registered trademark valid?

A registered trademark in India is valid for 10 years from the date of application and can be renewed indefinitely every 10 years.

Can I register a trademark without a company?

Yes. Individuals, sole proprietors, partnership firms, LLPs, companies, trusts and societies can all apply for a trademark in India.

What happens if my trademark gets an objection?

The examiner issues an examination report and you have 30 days to reply. Our trademark experts draft and file the reply for you and support you through the hearing if one is required.

What is a trademark class and how many should I choose?

Goods and services are grouped into 45 classes (1–34 for goods, 35–45 for services). You must file in every class that covers what you sell. Our experts recommend the right classes after the free trademark search.
