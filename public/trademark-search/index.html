What your trademark search report covers

A brand name can be refused if it clashes with an existing mark, even one that only sounds similar. Your report shows these risks before you spend on branding or filing.

  • Identical marks

    Exact matches of your brand name already filed or registered in India.

  • Similar-sounding names

    Phonetic matches and spelling variants that the Registry treats as confusingly similar.

  • Right trademark class

    Which of the 45 Nice classes fits your goods or services, and conflicts within it.

  • Status of conflicting marks

    Whether a clashing mark is registered, objected, opposed, abandoned or expired.

  • Availability outlook

    A plain-language view of how likely the name is to go through, and the main risks.

  • Expert next steps

    What to change if the name is risky, or how to file quickly if it looks clear.

Why search first

Check trademark availability before you build your brand

In India, trademark rights generally go to whoever files first. If someone already holds a similar mark, your application can be objected or opposed, and you may have to rebrand after spending on logos, packaging and marketing.

A trademark search takes minutes and tells you whether your brand name is likely to be available, which class to file in, and what to watch out for.

  • Avoid rejection: spot identical or similar marks before you file.
  • Save money: don't pay for branding on a name you can't protect.
  • Choose the right class: protect the goods or services you actually sell.
  • File with confidence: once filed, use the ™ symbol with your brand.

FAQs

Free trademark search: common questions

Is the trademark search really free?

Yes. The trademark search and the report are 100% free. No payment or card details are needed.

How will I receive my trademark search report?

Your report is sent to the WhatsApp number you enter. We verify the number with a one-time code first so the report reaches the right person.

What does the trademark search check?

We search the Indian trademark registry for identical marks, similar-sounding and similar-looking marks, and marks in the class that fits your business, and note the status of any conflicting marks.

Does a clear search mean my trademark will be registered?

No. The search is a preliminary check. Final registrability is decided by the Trade Marks Registry after examination, so a professional review is recommended before you file.

Which trademark class should I choose?

India follows the Nice Classification with 45 classes: 1–34 for goods and 35–45 for services. Tell us your business category and we will suggest the right class in your report.

What should I do after the search?

If your name looks available, file your trademark application early, because rights in India generally go to whoever files first. Bizpole can file it for you and you can use the ™ symbol once it is filed.
