    Why search first

    Check trademark availability before you build your brand

    In India, trademark rights generally go to whoever files first. If someone already holds a similar mark, your application can be objected or opposed, and you may have to rebrand after spending on logos, packaging and marketing.

    A trademark search takes minutes and tells you whether your brand name is likely to be available, which class to file in, and what to watch out for.

    • Avoid rejection: spot identical or similar marks before you file.
    • Save money: don't pay for branding on a name you can't protect.
    • Choose the right class: protect the goods or services you actually sell.
    • File with confidence: once filed, use the ™ symbol with your brand.

    FAQs

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the trademark search free?

    Yes. The trademark search and the report are 100% free. No payment or card details are needed.

    Does an available search result guarantee registration?

    No. A preliminary search is not a guarantee of registration. The result needs to be assessed in context, and the final decision rests with the Trade Marks Registry after examination.

    Can I search a logo as well as a brand name?

    Yes. Choose the Logo tab and upload your logo as a PNG or JPG (up to 2 MB). You can also add your logo to a brand-name search to check both together.

    What happens after I complete a search?

    Your report link is sent to the WhatsApp number you enter, usually within a minute. It shows your approval score, matching marks and their status, and a trademark expert can walk you through the findings and next steps.

    Which trademark class should I choose?

    India follows the Nice Classification with 45 classes: 1–34 for goods and 35–45 for services. Not sure which one? Search all classes, and our trademark expert will help you pick the right class for your business.

    Can Bizpole help with trademark registration?

    Yes. If your name looks available, Bizpole experts can file your trademark application for ₹3,000 incl. GST + Govt. fee, and you get your TM application number in 24–48 hours. File early, because rights in India generally go to whoever files first, and you can use the ™ symbol once it is filed.
