Private Limited Company Registration process

How to register a Private Limited Company — 6 steps

We handle every filing with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). You get updates in your dashboard.

  1. Consultation & name check

    We confirm your structure, check name availability and collect documents.

    Same day

  2. Digital signatures (DSC)

    Class 3 DSCs are issued for the proposed directors and shareholders.

    1–2 days

  3. Name reservation

    Your proposed name is filed with the MCA for approval (SPICe+ Part A).

    2–4 working days

  4. Incorporation filing

    SPICe+ Part B, e-MOA, e-AOA and AGILE-PRO-S are filed with DIN applications.

    After name approval

  5. Certificate of Incorporation

    The MCA issues your CoI with CIN, along with company PAN and TAN.

    Typically 5–7 working days

  6. Post-incorporation

    Open your current account; file commencement of business (INC-20A) within 180 days and appoint an auditor within 30 days.

    We remind you
Benefits

Why register as a Private Limited Company?

Limited liability

Shareholders' personal assets are protected — liability is limited to their shares.

Separate legal entity

The company owns assets, signs contracts and can sue or be sued in its own name.

Credibility

Customers, banks, investors and large clients prefer working with registered companies.

Easy to raise funds

Issue shares to investors and offer ESOPs — the preferred structure for startups.

Perpetual succession

The company continues even if directors or shareholders change.

Startup India ready

Eligible for DPIIT startup recognition and its tax and compliance benefits.

Documents required

Documents required for Private Limited Company registration

Upload clear scans or photos — our experts prepare and file everything else.

Directors & shareholders

  • PAN card (passport for foreign nationals)
  • Aadhaar card or passport
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Address proof — bank statement or utility bill (not older than 2 months)
  • Email ID and mobile number

Registered office

  • Proof of registered office address (ownership document or occupancy proof)
  • Rent agreement or lease deed, if rented
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the property owner
  • Recent electricity or other utility bill

FAQs

Private Limited Company Registration — frequently asked questions

How much does Private Limited Company registration cost?

Our all-inclusive price — professional fee, DSC, GST, government fee and stamp duty — starts at ₹10,000 and depends on your state, because stamp duty is set by each state. Pick your state in the pricing section to see the exact amount.

How long does it take to register a Private Limited Company?

Typically 10–15 working days from receiving complete documents, subject to name approval and MCA processing times.

Is there a minimum capital requirement?

No. There is no minimum paid-up capital for a Private Limited Company in India; you can start with any amount.

How many directors and shareholders are needed?

At least 2 directors and 2 shareholders (the same people can be both). At least one director must be resident in India. A Private Limited Company can have up to 200 shareholders.

Can NRIs or foreign nationals be directors?

Yes. NRIs and foreign nationals can be directors and shareholders, provided at least one director is resident in India.

What do I need to do after incorporation?

Open a current account, file the commencement of business declaration (INC-20A) within 180 days, appoint the first auditor within 30 days, and complete annual ROC filings (AOC-4, MGT-7) and income tax returns every year.

Why is the price different in my state?

Stamp duty on the MOA, AOA and incorporation is fixed by each state government, and it is included in our all-inclusive price. That is why the total differs from state to state.
